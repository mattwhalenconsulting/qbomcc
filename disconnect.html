Disconnecting the app

The Personal QuickBooks Assistant is a private application for its owner only. To revoke its access to a QuickBooks Online company:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Open the apps area of the account and find this application in the list of connected apps.
  3. Choose to disconnect it.

After that, the stored tokens stop working. The locally stored credentials file can then be deleted from the owner's computer.
